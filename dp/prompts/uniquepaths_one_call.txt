================================================================================
PROMPT 1: One Recursive Call -> uniquepaths_one_call.png
================================================================================

Create a landscape hand-drawn educational infographic, 1536x1024.

STYLE: a bright, fun classroom poster for a 10 year old, in friendly hand-drawn
marker lettering.
- Page background: soft warm cream with a faint pale dotted grid, plus a few
  small confetti dots and doodle stars in the empty areas.
- Every major box has a SOLID SATURATED colored header bar with its title in
  bold WHITE lettering, a soft pastel tint filling the body, a thick rounded
  outline and a soft drop shadow.
- Thick COLORED arrows matching the block they point to, big friendly
  arrowheads.
- Two or three BIG cute cartoon doodles with smiling faces and rosy cheeks,
  tucked into the margins so they never overlap text.
- A yellow sticky note with a red pushpin for the example.
- All lettering large, dark and crisp on the pale fills. Never shrink text.

HOW THE DATA IS DRAWN, VERY IMPORTANT:
Never draw the input as a row of individually coloured cells, and never write a
row of index numbers under it. That is visual noise. Instead:
- The input is ONE long flat rounded bar in plain neutral GREY, empty, with
  nothing written along it. Keep the bar LONG. The whole effect depends on one
  small bright square sitting on a long empty bar.
- Exactly ONE square sticks out of that bar: the current element, filled BRIGHT
  PURPLE with its value in bold white, and its index written under it ONLY in
  the CURRENT CALL box.
- Any part already used or dropped is pale grey, hatched, and crossed out.
- Never highlight two squares on the same bar.

STEP BADGES: each step gets a big round numbered badge in the colour of that
step, on the top left corner of its block. The same numbers appear beside the
matching lines in the code panel.

TITLE across the top, very large, first words purple, rest black:
   "UNIQUE PATHS  -  ONE CALL"

TOP LEFT: a yellow sticky note with a red pushpin and a small target doodle:
   "THE EXAMPLE"

   "for a 3 by 7 grid  ->  answer 28"

   "we are sitting at the top left corner."

   On its right:
      A small grid of plain grey squares, 3 rows by 7 columns, drawn flat with

      labelled "finish". Every other square is plain empty grey.

TOP RIGHT: a box with a dark slate header bar reading "THE CODE". Very pale
