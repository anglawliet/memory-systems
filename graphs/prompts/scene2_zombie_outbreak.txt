THE TRICK: len(queue) = 2 at the START. The for loop processes EXACTLY 2.
Market and School go to the BACK of the queue. They wait for next wave!

Visual: Deck's scoreboard as a horizontal bar. A dotted line separates "THIS wave" from "NEXT wave." The for loop processes everything LEFT of the line.

Code (LARGE, annotated):
  while queue:
      for _ in range(len(queue)):    # THIS wave's size, frozen!
          node = queue.popleft()
          for nei in graph[node]:
              if nei not in visited:
                  visited.add(nei)
                  queue.append(nei)  # added to BACK = next wave
      waves += 1                     # wave done!
